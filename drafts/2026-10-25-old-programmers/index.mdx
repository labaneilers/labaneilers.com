There's a familiar trope about prejudice against aging technologists. It's a young person's industry, and older individual contributors are viewed as stogy, curmudgeonly relics- they're stuck in their ways, out of touch with the vibe, always going on about cache alignment and disassembly.

I know people who feel like they've been labeled like this. they feel like it's unfair
Some of them deserve it
there's a difference between being old vs being stubborn and closed minded

octogenerian politicians taking up space

values misalignment

I love hanging out with my genZ colleauges
they teach me fun terms like temu
they get me to think about things from new angles- question stuff I take for granted
with agents, what are PRs for

While I certainly know some folks that fit this stereotype, I've never been too afraid it being applied to me. I have to admit I share some this prejudice. I may be nearing my own sell-by date, but I also have a limited appetite for people who are incalcitrant and unadaptable. I mean, it's pretty objectively a *huge liability* in this game.

For those of us who've been able to retain our curiosity and open-mindedness, there are also some advantages to age. Experience and instincts correlate pretty strongly to one's duration, and those things come in pretty handy- especially these days, where so many of our assumptions about what it means to be productive are being overturned by the rise of our synthetic little helpers.

now that i'm in the in-group, maybe I'm allowed to be a bit more critical
